# Historical image cache

`live-qr.png` encodes `https://live.scalableweb.dev/`. Generated with Python qrcode 8.2, medium error correction, 12 pixels/module and a four-module quiet zone. Independently decoded with OpenCV; no tracking redirect.

`mundaneum-drawers.jpg` is an unmodified copy of **Drawers**, photographed by fdecomite on 23 February 2011. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drawers.jpg), originally [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/21649179@N00/5471946770). License: [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Downloaded 15 September 2026. The photographed drawers are in Mons; the photo does not date from Otlet’s 1895 catalogue project.

`ticket-qr.png` is the unchanged registration QR supplied by the lecturer in Obsidian at `Lectures/Web Development 2026/Assets/ticket-qr.png`. It accompanies the student invitation and is not a historical image.

Only the expressly licensed Mundaneum photograph is redistributed among the historical images. Other historical images retain their source URLs and visible explanatory captions, so the teaching claim remains available if a remote image fails. They are not licensed by this repository. In particular, [CERN’s terms](https://copyright.web.cern.ch/) permit educational use with credit but restrict third-party redistribution; no general redistribution permission is assumed for the museum and archive screenshots.

This directory is explicitly copied to the audience deployment. Do not put private notes or unreviewed assets here.
