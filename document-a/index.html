01 / The essentials

SDLCAI brings practitioners, researchers, engineering leaders, and product teams together to discuss AI across the software development lifecycle. Expect talks, posters, and a panel, with attention to engineering quality and practical adoption.

Format

02 / What will the day explore?

  • AI in everyday engineering. Adoption across planning, implementation, maintenance, and delivery.
  • Quality under automation. Evaluation, testing, and review when AI contributes to the work.
  • Teams and governance. Developer experience, leadership, and organizational change.
